## Introduction

GameLaunch ("we", "us", or "our") is a directory of trending games, codes, secrets, release dates and guides. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the site and how we use it.

## Information We Collect

1. **Account Information** — If you create an account, we store your name, email address and profile image (for example, from Google or GitHub sign-in).
2. **Submissions** — Game submissions and support tickets you send us, including any links or text you provide.
3. **Usage Data** — Pages visited, search terms entered on the site, referring pages, device and browser type, collected through cookies and privacy-friendly analytics.

## How We Use Information

- Operate, maintain and improve the directory and its guides.
- Review game submissions and respond to support requests.
- Understand which games and topics are popular so we can cover them.
- Protect the site against abuse, spam and fraud.

## Outbound Links

Our pages link to official game sites, app stores and third-party portals. Those sites have their own privacy practices; we are not responsible for their content or policies.

## Cookies

We use essential cookies for sign-in and language preferences, and analytics cookies to understand site usage. You can block cookies in your browser; some features (like signing in) may stop working.

## Data Retention and Deletion

We keep account data while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time by opening a support ticket.

## Children

The directory covers games popular with all ages, but accounts are not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The "updated" date above shows the latest revision.

## Contact

Questions about privacy? Open a support ticket from your account settings.
