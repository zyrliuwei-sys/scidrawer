## Introduction

SciDrawer AI ("we", "us", or "our") provides an AI-powered platform for generating scientific figures for research papers, academic books, and presentations. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use scidrawer.ai or any related services we operate.

## Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide and improve the service:

1. **Account Information** — Your name, email address, and password hash when you create an account.
2. **Generation Inputs** — The text prompts, sketches, and reference images you submit to generate scientific figures. We process these inputs to produce your outputs and to improve service quality.
3. **Generated Outputs** — The scientific figures, diagrams, and other images you create through the service, along with associated metadata such as generation parameters and timestamps.
4. **Usage Data** — Information about how you interact with the service, including features accessed, generation counts, and interaction patterns.
5. **Device Information** — Device type, operating system, and browser type, used to optimize compatibility and performance.
6. **Cookies** — Small data files stored on your device to remember preferences and keep you signed in.
7. **Payment Information** — Billing details handled by trusted third-party payment processors (such as Stripe). We do not store full card numbers on our servers.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the data we collect to:

- Provide, maintain, and improve SciDrawer AI's figure-generation capabilities.
- Process transactions and send related confirmations.
- Send technical notices, security alerts, and support messages.
- Respond to your questions, feedback, and support requests.
- Detect, prevent, and address fraud and abuse.
- Comply with legal obligations.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data only in these limited cases:

- With service providers who help us operate the service (such as cloud storage, payment processors, and AI model providers), under confidentiality agreements.
- To comply with legal obligations or respond to lawful requests from public authorities.
- To protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of our users.

## Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit, access controls, and regular reviews. No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, but we work continuously to protect your information.

## Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, export, or delete your personal information and your generation history. You can do this through your account settings or by contacting us at the email address below.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, sending a notice through the service.
